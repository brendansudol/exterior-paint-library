Search coverage & limitations

Searched public web and image results for the color name, SW 9132/SW9132 variants, exterior/siding/stucco/house/door terms, painter portfolios, social posts, image credits, decor articles and property listings. Followed original sources where available and inspected the collected images. Deduplicated identical files and known reposts; retained distinct angles, seasonal views and labeled comparison panels. A source-confirmed label means the project source names the paint; it is not a laboratory verification of paint or photographic color accuracy. Private, deleted, unindexed and inaccessible posts may contain further photos. This is a thorough public-web collection, not a guarantee of every image in existence. The October 9 follow-up traced original image credits into complete builder albums, revisited homeowner discussion replies, and cross-checked Home Town production paint schedules against published finished-house photos. It also checked additional contractor results, social posts, Houzz discussions, property listings and decor-site images. The Legacy Lake Homes builder album confirms the pictured house, but does not independently confirm its paint color. Published television stills and construction-stage photos are labeled.
