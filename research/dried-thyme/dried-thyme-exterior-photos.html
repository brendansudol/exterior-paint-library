Research notes   Source manifest

What qualifies—and what does not

Source-confirmed: a painter, project designer, homeowner or production company names the applied color. Attributed: an image is labeled by a secondary publication or Pinterest user, without independent confirmation from the original project. Exterior accents: the color is on a door, shutter, porch floor, skirting or trim detail. The rest of the house uses other finishes. Each group states whether the color evidence is primary or secondary.

Photographs show real projects according to their source and visual content. This research cannot certify camera processing or physical paint chemistry. No image has been recolored for this collection. Screens, lighting, shade and camera processing all affect appearance.

Search coverage and limits

Known duplicate sizes, crops and reposts were removed. Distinct angles, useful exterior details, seasonal views and labeled work stages remain. The 39-photo main collection includes 13 confirmed body-color views, 11 additional attributed exterior views and 15 accent views. The one unverified Craftsman is a research lead outside that count.
