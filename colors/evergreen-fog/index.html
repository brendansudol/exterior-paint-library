Search coverage & limitations

Searched public web and image results for Evergreen Fog, SW 9130/SW9130, house, exterior, siding, stucco, painting, before/after and door terms. Checked contractor portfolios, builder articles, homeowner posts, manufacturer features, Houzz albums, Instagram carousels, Reddit leads and secondary design publications. Traced credits where possible, inspected collected images, removed known reposts and before-only photos, and kept distinct angles and labeled comparison/stage images. Source-confirmed means an original project source names the applied paint; it is not a guarantee of paint chemistry or camera color accuracy. The nine confirmed projects include 25 image assets; one asset is a before/after panel. Additional attributions include one project-palette inference. Private, deleted, unindexed, login-restricted and otherwise inaccessible posts may contain more photographs. This is a thorough public-web collection, not a claim to every photo in existence.
