Search coverage & limitations

Searched public text and image results using Moody Blue, Sherwin-Williams, SW 6221/SW6221, exterior, siding, house, painting, before/after, homeowner, door and shutter terms. Checked contractor portfolios, designer projects, homeowner discussions and updates, public social posts, Houzz paint answers, credited design publications, Pinterest reposts and manufacturer material. Followed project photos and source credits, visually inspected the saved images, and removed known duplicate compositions. Source-confirmed body color requires an original professional or homeowner statement; secondary body attributions have a separate category. Exterior accents have their own category, with primary versus secondary evidence explained in each group. Different angles remain, but image counts are not house counts. Some accent photos show the painted detail only at a distance or in shadow. No recoloring or generated imagery was introduced. This is a thorough public-web collection, not a guarantee of every photograph in existence: private, deleted, unindexed, login-restricted or inaccessible posts may contain more.
