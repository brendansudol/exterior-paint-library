Research notes   Source manifest

What qualifies—and what does not

Builder-identified: the project professional names the paint. The Old Drake set has a visual discrepancy with parts of its written palette, explicitly noted above. Attributed: an image is labeled by a secondary publication or Pinterest user, without independent confirmation from the original project. Exterior accents: the color is on a door or entry frame. Evidence may be primary or secondary, as stated for each set.

Photographs show real projects according to their source and visual content. This research cannot certify camera processing or physical paint chemistry. No image has been recolored for this collection. Screens, lighting, shade and camera processing all affect appearance.

Search coverage and limits

Known duplicate sizes and reposts are counted once. Distinct angles, evening views and construction stages remain. The main collection has 20 body/siding photos and eight door/entry photos. Ten body photos were added in the follow-up search; they show two existing houses from additional angles. Two conflicting-color bungalow references are separate. The main collection is not a claim that all 28 images have independent primary confirmation.
