Research notes   Source manifest

What qualifies—and what does not

Source-confirmed: a painter, builder, project designer or homeowner names the applied color. Attributed: an image has a secondary publication’s color label, or its individual color is inferred from a documented multi-color project palette. Exterior accents: the color is on doors, shutters or an enclosed porch; check each project’s evidence note.

Photographs show real projects according to their source and visual content. This research cannot certify camera processing or physical paint chemistry. No image has been recolored for this collection. Screens, lighting, shade and camera processing all affect appearance.
