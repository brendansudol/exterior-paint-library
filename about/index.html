What the labels mean

Source-confirmed body

A painter, designer, homeowner, builder or production company names the paint used on the house body. This is confirmation from the source, not a physical test of paint chemistry.

Secondary attribution

A publication or social post labels the pictured color, but an original project paint statement was not recovered. These images remain useful references with a lower level of certainty.

Doors & accents

The color appears on a door, shutter, porch element or other accent. The main siding may use a different paint color. Read the palette and evidence for each project.

Uncertain examples & sample boards

These appear only under Everything, with separate labels. They are excluded from the body and siding totals. Uncertain examples can include unresolved paint stages, proposed specifications or digitally altered listing elements.

How the photos were collected

The research covered public web and image search, painter and builder portfolios, homeowner posts, project albums, property listings and design publications. Where possible, reposts were traced back to original project sources. Known duplicate images were removed within each collection; distinct angles and labeled comparison panels were retained.

Each color’s original research gallery and source manifest remain available. The manifests record image URLs, original source pages, evidence notes and checksums for the downloaded files.

Reading color in a photograph

Lighting, surroundings, camera processing and your screen affect what you see. Images have not been recolored for this site. Smaller previews are resized and compressed for browsing; the viewer links to the original downloaded image. Paint swatches are approximate navigation aids. Try a physical sample outdoors before deciding.
