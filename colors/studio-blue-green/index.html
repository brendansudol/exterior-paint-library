Search coverage & limitations

Broad public-web research using Studio Blue Green, Sherwin-Williams, SW 0047/SW0047 and exterior, house, siding, stucco, porch, door, trim, painting and before/after terms. Reviewed text and image results, seven pages of the exact-color exterior search, builder and painter sources, homeowner discussions, Houzz photos and paint answers, public social references, Pinterest, historic blogs, real-estate listings, manufacturer pages and architectural packets. Expanded the nine-photo reference database, screened the Southport listing’s 47 images, and traced the seven Old Drake project entries (two exteriors, five interiors). Retained photographs were visually inspected; known duplicate compositions, sizes and reposts were removed. The expanded 28-photo main collection contains two builder-identified body views, eighteen secondary body attributions and eight exterior-door/entry views. The follow-up traced original designer and homeowner accounts, inspected Sarah Karon’s exterior portfolio, and screened all 43 listing photographs of the porch house. Ten additional siding/body photos were retained: eight listing views and one original homeowner photo of the porch house, plus one designer-portfolio close-up of the existing Atlanta bungalow. These are additional views of two existing houses, not ten new houses. Same-house visual matching extends the secondary paint attribution; the additional source pages do not independently confirm SW 0047. Six of those eight door views have primary homeowner or listing evidence; two rely on secondary publications. Two photographs of Bungalow 404 have conflicting color identifications and remain outside the main count. Source groups are not a count of unique houses. No recoloring or AI imagery was introduced. Source identification is not physical verification of paint or camera color. This is a thorough public-web collection, not a guarantee of every photograph ever posted; private, deleted, unindexed and inaccessible sources may contain more.
