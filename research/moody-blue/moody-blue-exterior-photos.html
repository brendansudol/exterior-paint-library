Research notes   Source manifest

What qualifies—and what does not

Source-confirmed: a painter, project designer, homeowner or production company names the applied color. Attributed: an image is labeled by a secondary publication or Pinterest user, without independent confirmation from the original project. Exterior accents: the color is on a door, shutter, garage door or porch ceiling; the rest of the house may use other colors. Each accent project states whether its color evidence is primary or secondary.

Photographs show real projects according to their source and visual content. This research cannot certify camera processing or physical paint chemistry. No image has been recolored for this collection. Screens, lighting, shade and camera processing all affect appearance.

Search coverage and limits

Known duplicate sizes and reposts were removed. Distinct angles and useful exterior details remain. The accent set is separate from the body-color photos; it includes some distant, shaded and nighttime views, clearly labeled. Each group explains which surface is Moody Blue and how the color was identified.
