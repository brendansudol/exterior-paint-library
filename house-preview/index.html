About these previews & color references

22 sampled appearances from 21 real photographs. Each variation comes from the median RGB values of the marked body-color patch in its source photograph. We avoid trim, windows, roof and plants. The labels describe visible conditions; they are not measured lighting data. Some references use vertical siding, shingles or stucco rather than lap siding.

All photo variations use the same completed Acacia Haze renovation image for each view. A manually traced siding mask and a deterministic color transfer hold the house, wood door, trim and surroundings fixed. The transfer maps the sample to a brighter ordinary siding tone in that view, retaining the base image’s relative light and shadow. It does not estimate paint reflectance, correct source white balance or simulate new weather. Masks are approximate at fine foliage and railing edges.

Studio Blue Green has weaker evidence. Its two samples come from two views of one builder-attributed project. The photographed trim conflicts with the written palette. These examples are clearly flagged and should not establish an expected paint color.

The earlier AI interpretations remain available under “Earlier AI preview.” The renovation assumes horizontal lap siding throughout, existing white trim and gray foundation, and an illustrative natural-wood door. Compare real painted siding samples at your house before deciding.
